## Frozen Scientific Boundary

- Geometry defines reproducible local route structure and diagnostic support;
  it is not a theorem or a direct compression-safety predictor.
- Controlled feedback updates route state over repeated interactions and can
  support conditional recovery; it is not a universal first-pass unseen-query
  advantage or the sole source of fused quality.
- Dense and BM25 provide the rescue surface. Independent calibration chooses
  the final evidence-context budget.
- The measured efficiency endpoint is final evidence-input tokens. It may be
  converted into a conditional input-price component under a declared price,
  but it is not an end-to-end latency, energy, memory, or total-cost result.
- Task73 was prospectively specified before data download and outcome
  inspection, but it was not registered on a public immutable platform.
